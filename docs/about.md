# Whats PhosoAI
PhosoAI is an LLM based chatbot whose goal is to provide access to food security and agriculture productivity information in a user friendly manner by leveraging LLMs capability digest large documents and quantitative tabular data and provide responses in natural language to audiences who can speak English, those who cant read and write.
## Project Country
The current iteration of this project is set in Malawi. Its a country in east southern Africa with a population of about 20 million people. Out of these people, only about 70% are literate, furthermore for those who can read and write, English is not their mother tongue, as such only 30-40% of the population can sufficiently comprehend in English.

## What Problem are We Solving?
In Malawi, the Ministry of Agriculture and the NSO collects massive amount data on food prices, agriculture productivity and price of other essential commodities.However, a regular person doesnt comprehend English very well or is inept at reading tables do not have access to this information because of a multitude of obstacles:
- **Language obstacles**. This information is usually available in English only
- **Device access obstacles**. This information is avaibale on websites requiring a computer for easy and comfotable access
- **Too technical for the layman**. The information is often available in tabular format making it hard for a regular person with little education to access.
- **Too bulky**. If information is not in tabular format, then its available in bulky reports where a user has to sift through several hundreds of pages to find perhaps one number or indicator they are looking for. 

When combined, all these factors make it very hard for a regular person on the street to find answers to simple food security related questions such as 
1. Where can I find cheap maize without relying on word of mouth
2. Where can I sell my product?

## Our Solution
We take advantage of LLMs to democratize access to food prices, agriculture commodities prices, agriculture productivity (harvest statistics), food availability and other pertinent information for Agriculture such as climate and weather information. We do it in the following way.
1. **Text2SQL.** Leverage this technique to allow users to ask questions in natural language (e.g., where can i find cheap maize) but yet access tabular data with historical price information for all major markets of the country.
2. **Use local language**. Allow users to ask questions in CHichewa, the major language in the country.
3. **Whatsapp app**. Allow users to use Whatsapp to ask these questions becuase it has become a popular platform among locals.
